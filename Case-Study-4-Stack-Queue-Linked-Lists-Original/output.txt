STACK OF PLATES (LIFO)
Push: Plate 1
Push: Plate 2
Push: Plate 3
Top to bottom: Plate 3 -> Plate 2 -> Plate 1
Peek: Plate 3
Pop: Plate 3
After pop: Plate 2 -> Plate 1

CUSTOMER QUEUE (FIFO)
Join: Customer A
Join: Customer B
Join: Customer C
Front to rear: Customer A -> Customer B -> Customer C
Peek: Customer A
Serve: Customer A
After serving: Customer B -> Customer C
